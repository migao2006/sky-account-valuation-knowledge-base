# Days of Bloom FAQ 1278 core-four cohort

This bounded, replayable cohort records only the four individually named new Days of Bloom 2024 offers in official FAQ 1278, their stated costs, and the historical Pacific window from 2024-03-25 through 2024-04-14.

All four official titles are byte-identical to the pinned SkyGame-Data 1.3.4 vendor titles. Returning items are outside this cohort. Current availability, permanence, first release, Traditional Chinese names, visual identity, resale claims, and account ownership remain unasserted.

Replay with `python tools/normalize/apply_days_of_bloom_faq1278_core_four_cohort.py --apply`; ordinary verification runs without `--apply`.
